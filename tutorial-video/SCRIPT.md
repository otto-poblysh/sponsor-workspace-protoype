# Tutorial Video Script: Labour Market Portal

**Format**: UI screencast with cursor tracking, subtle highlights, and bottom-third subtitles.
**Pacing**: Relaxed tutorial pace. Mouse movements are deliberate and steady, giving the viewer time to read subtitles and observe the UI changes.

---

## Segment 1: Dashboard Overview & Filtering (Director Persona)

**[00:00 - 00:04]**
- **Visual Action**: Fade in on the `Dashboard` (`index.html`). The mouse cursor enters from the bottom right and moves towards the top KPI cards.
- **Subtitle**: "Welcome to the Labour Market Portal."

**[00:04 - 00:09]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse smoothly hovers over the 'Registered Employers' card, then the 'Active Vacancies' card. A subtle highlight state appears on hover.
- **Subtitle**: "Start by reviewing high-level national employment metrics."

**[00:09 - 00:15]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse moves to the 'Sector' filter dropdown at the top of the page. Clicks the dropdown, scrolls slightly, and selects 'Construction'.
- **Subtitle**: "When you need to track sector-specific growth, filter the national dashboard in seconds."

**[00:15 - 00:19]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse clicks the 'Apply' button. The dashboard charts and KPI cards refresh with a skeleton loading state briefly, then show the filtered data.
- **Subtitle**: "The metrics instantly update to reflect your selected criteria."

---

## Segment 2: Verifying an Employer (Compliance Officer Persona)

**[00:19 - 00:23]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse moves to the left sidebar and clicks on 'Registered Employers' (`entities.html`).
- **Subtitle**: "Managing company compliance is straightforward."

**[00:23 - 00:28]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse moves over the data table. Hovers over the 'Status' column to locate an employer marked 'Pending' (e.g., Annobón Eco Tours).
- **Subtitle**: "Locate new employer registrations that are pending review."

**[00:28 - 00:34]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse clicks the context menu (three dots) next to the pending employer and selects 'Verify Employer'. A confirmation modal appears.
- **Subtitle**: "When reviewing new registrations, quickly verify employers to grant them reporting access."

**[00:34 - 00:38]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse clicks 'Confirm' in the modal. A green success toast notification appears at the top right.
- **Subtitle**: "The employer is now verified and notified automatically."

---

## Segment 3: Analyzing Skill Gaps (Director Persona)

**[00:38 - 00:42]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse moves to the sidebar and clicks 'Skill Gap Report' (`skill-gap.html`).
- **Subtitle**: "Use the platform to inform your vocational training strategies."

**[00:42 - 00:49]**
- **Visual Action**: The page loads showing the skills demand charts. Mouse moves to the 'Hard-to-fill roles' bar chart and hovers over the top bar (e.g., 'Industrial Electrician'), revealing a tooltip with exact numbers.
- **Subtitle**: "When planning training budgets, identify roles with high employer demand and low worker supply."

---

## Segment 4: Generating a Report (Analyst Persona)

**[00:49 - 00:53]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse moves to the sidebar and clicks 'Labour Market Reports' (`general-report.html`).
- **Subtitle**: "Reporting is automated and always up-to-date."

**[00:53 - 01:00]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse clicks on the 'Monthly Labour Summary' report card. Selects the 'PDF' format radio button, and clicks the 'Generate Report' button.
- **Subtitle**: "When briefing leadership, generate comprehensive, standardized reports in minutes."

**[01:00 - 01:04]**
- **Visual Action**: A loading spinner appears on the button, followed by a 'Download Ready' notification. Mouse clicks 'Download'.
- **Subtitle**: "Your report is ready to share immediately."

---

## Segment 5: Auditing Activity (Compliance Officer Persona)

**[01:04 - 01:08]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse moves to the sidebar and clicks 'Activity Logs' (`activity-logs.html`).
- **Subtitle**: "Every action taken on the portal is securely recorded."

**[01:08 - 01:15]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse scrolls down the list of recent activities. Clicks on a specific log entry (e.g., 'Status changed to Verified'). An expanded view opens showing the exact timestamp and user.
- **Subtitle**: "When maintaining oversight, trace every important action through immutable activity logs."

**[01:15 - 01:20]**
- **Visual Action**: Mouse pans back to the center of the screen as the video fades to black. Ministry of Labour logo appears.
- **Subtitle**: "Empowering smarter employment decisions." (Fade out)
