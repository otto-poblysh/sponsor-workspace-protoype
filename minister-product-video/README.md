# Ministry of Labour Product Film

This directory contains the English-first production package for a silent HyperFrames product film demonstrating the Equatorial Guinea Ministry of Labour Market Portal.

## Purpose

The film is designed to help the Minister and senior Ministry stakeholders understand how the platform turns private-sector employment data into faster reporting, stronger oversight, better training priorities, and more informed employment decisions.

The first production language is English. English review and approval must be completed before Spanish translation or Spanish implementation begins.

## Canonical documents

1. `PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` — scope, workflow, ownership, gates, risks, and production sequence.
2. `DESIGN.md` — visual identity and composition constraints.
3. `CONTENT_MATRIX.md` — audience roles and the feature–advantage–benefit argument.
4. `SCRIPT.md` — timed English on-screen copy for the silent master film.
5. `STORYBOARD.md` — beat-by-beat visual, motion, transition, music, and asset direction.
6. `ASSET_AUDIT.md` — source-page and asset capture plan.
7. `REVIEW_PLAN.md` — internal review rounds, acceptance criteria, and sign-off process.
8. `LOCALIZATION_PLAN.md` — controlled English-to-Spanish workflow after English approval.
