# English On-Screen Script

Status: First production draft — not yet copy locked

Format: Silent product film with gentle instrumental music and no voiceover.

Target duration: 88 seconds.

Reading rule: each headline must be understandable without the support line. Support copy may deepen the message but must never be required to follow the story.

## Global persistent labels

- `MINISTRY OF LABOUR`
- `LABOUR MARKET PORTAL`
- `Illustrative demo data`

The illustrative-data label should appear discreetly in beats containing statistics. It does not need to appear on the closing card.

**Headline:**

**Resolve line:**

> One Ministry needs one clear national view.
