# Audience and Feature–Advantage–Benefit Matrix

Status: English review draft

## Core Ministry outcome

The platform gives the Ministry a consolidated and accountable view of private-sector employment activity so leadership and operational teams can produce reports faster, identify labour-market constraints, and direct employment and training interventions using evidence.

## Role-based value

| Ministry role | Key question | Platform capability | Operational advantage | Ministry benefit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minister / senior leadership | What is happening in private-sector employment now? | National dashboard with employers, vacancies, seekers, applications, trends, and shortages | A concise briefing can be obtained without combining several spreadsheets | Faster, better-informed leadership decisions |
| Director of Employment | Where are vacancies and placements growing or slowing? | Sector, province, period, status, and workforce-size filters | Teams can isolate a policy question in seconds | Targeted employment programmes and employer engagement |
| Director of Training / vocational education | Which capabilities are employers unable to find? | Skill demand, worker supply, shortage states, and hard-to-fill roles | Training gaps come directly from employer demand and seeker profiles | Training investment aligns with real labour-market need |
| Labour-market analyst | How can we produce recurring evidence quickly? | Aggregated dashboards, templates, and PDF/Excel/CSV exports | Reusable reports reduce manual consolidation and formatting | Shorter reporting cycles and more time for analysis |
| Employer-registration / compliance officer | Which companies are registered, verified, inactive, or pending? | Employer registry, verification status, size, sector, and onboarding progress | One controlled workflow replaces scattered employer lists | Better reporting coverage, data quality, and compliance follow-up |
| Regional officer | What is happening in my province? | Provincial coverage, regional filters, and scoped access | Regional staff can work from the same national system with appropriate boundaries | Consistent national data with local accountability |
| Portal administrator | Who can view, edit, export, or administer information? | Ministry roles, invitations, status controls, and permissions | Access is organised by function rather than shared credentials | Safer collaboration and clearer responsibility |
| Audit / governance reviewer | Who changed what and when? | Immutable activity logs and notifications | Important actions are traceable without separate manual logs | Stronger oversight, institutional trust, and audit readiness |

## Feature–advantage–benefit messages for the film

### 1. National labour-market dashboard

- **Feature:** Consolidated KPIs for registered employers, active vacancies, job seekers, and applications.
- **Advantage:** Leadership sees related indicators in one place and can compare movement over time.
- **Benefit:** The Minister receives a current national briefing faster.
- **Proof shown:** 386 registered employers, 1,126 active vacancies, 18,742 registered job seekers, and 6,408 applications.
- **Film message:** “Now, the Ministry can see the national picture.”

### 2. Precise filters

- **Feature:** Filter by period, sector, province, employer status, and workforce size.
- **Advantage:** Users can move from a national total to a specific policy question without requesting a new spreadsheet.
- **Benefit:** Ministry teams respond to sectoral and regional needs more quickly.
- **Proof shown:** Construction + Bata filter selection and the resulting focused dataset.
- **Film message:** “Ask a precise question—and see the answer immediately.”

### 3. Registered employer management

- **Feature:** Employer registration, verification, suspension, sector, size, and onboarding progress.
- **Advantage:** Private-sector participation is organised through a controlled reporting workflow.
- **Benefit:** The Ministry improves reporting coverage, employer follow-up, and data trust.
- **Proof shown:** Bioko Marine Services verified; Litoral Obras onboarding; Annobón Eco Tours pending review.
- **Film message:** “Build a trusted network of reporting employers.”

### 4. Vacancies and recruitment outcomes

- **Feature:** Active vacancies, applications, hard-to-fill roles, and the applicant-to-hired funnel.
- **Advantage:** Teams can see where recruitment demand exists and where applicants drop out.
- **Benefit:** The Ministry can focus employer support and placement initiatives where they matter most.
- **Proof shown:** 1,126 vacancies, 186 hard-to-fill roles, and 814 hires from 6,408 applications.
- **Film message:** “See where recruitment moves—and where it stalls.”

### 5. Skills-gap intelligence

- **Feature:** Employer skill demand compared with registered worker supply and shortage classifications.
- **Advantage:** Training priorities are based on observable vacancy demand rather than anecdote alone.
- **Benefit:** Training budgets and programmes can target industrial electrical systems, welding, equipment maintenance, hospitality, and other constrained areas.
- **Proof shown:** Shortage and tight-supply indicators from the current Skill Gap Report.
- **Film message:** “Turn employer demand into training priorities.”

### 6. Labour-market reports

- **Feature:** Monthly labour summary, employer compliance, private-sector vacancy, recruitment outcome, skills-demand, and inactive-employer reports.
- **Advantage:** Analysts select a format and generate a consistent report without rebuilding it manually.
- **Benefit:** Ministerial briefings that once required days of consolidation can be prepared in minutes.
- **Proof shown:** PDF, Excel, and CSV report-generation workflow.
- **Film message:** “Generate clear ministerial reports in minutes.”

### 7. Role-based access

- **Feature:** Director, Administrator, Regional Officer, and Labour Analyst roles.
- **Advantage:** Different teams work in the same system with access aligned to their responsibilities.
- **Benefit:** Collaboration becomes safer and duplication is reduced.
- **Proof shown:** Ministry Users table and role selections.
- **Film message:** “Give every team the access it needs.”

### 8. Accountability and notifications

- **Feature:** Immutable activity history, administrative notifications, and regional coverage settings.
- **Advantage:** The Ministry can trace actions, follow reporting events, and manage participation consistently.
- **Benefit:** Stronger governance and confidence in the reporting process.
- **Proof shown:** Employer verification, user invitation, regional change, and report-generation log entries.
- **Film message:** “Keep every important action visible and accountable.”

## Claims policy

- Platform figures are illustrative demo data and must not be represented as official national statistics.
- “In minutes” describes the demonstrated report-generation workflow, not a measured service-level guarantee.
- The film may say the system “supports” or “helps” decision-making; it should not claim that the software makes policy decisions.
- The film should demonstrate only functions represented in the current prototype.
- Benefits should be framed institutionally: visibility, speed, alignment, coverage, accountability, and coordination.

## Priority hierarchy if duration must be reduced

1. Dashboard and national visibility
2. Reports and faster briefing production
3. Skills gaps and training priorities
4. Employer verification and reporting coverage
5. Vacancies and recruitment outcomes
6. Role-based access and accountability
7. Regional coverage detail

The opening and closing should remain even in a shorter edit.
